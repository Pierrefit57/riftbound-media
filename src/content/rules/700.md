## 700. Additional Rules

### 701. Buffs
702. Objects placed on Units.
    * 702.2. Can be added or spent.
    * 703. Each Buff contributes **+1 Might**.

### 706. Mighty
708. A Unit "**is Mighty**" if its Might is 5 or greater.

### 712. Bonus Damage
713. Intrinsic property granted to Deal actions. Increases damage dealt.

### 716. Attachment
717. **Attaching**: Linking two cards. One becomes **Attached**, one becomes **Top-Most Card**.
718. **Attached** state:
    * Rules Text is **Inactive**.
    * Effect Text abilities checked/appended to Top-Most Card.
    * Might Bonus modulates Top-Most Card.
719. **Top-Most Card**: The card on top. Moves together with attached cards.
720. **Inactive**: Text is ignored/not processed.

### 726. Keywords
730. **Keyword Glossary**
    * 731. **Accelerate** (Unit): Pay cost to enter Ready instead of Exhausted.
    * 732. **Action** (Permissive): Can be played/activated during **Showdowns** (even on opponent's turn).
    * 733. **Assault [X]** (Passive): While attacking, I have +X Might.
    * 734. **Deathknell** (Triggered): "When I die..."
    * 735. **Deflect [X]** (Passive): Tax cost [X] to be targeted by enemy spells/abilities.
    * 736. **Ganking** (Passive): Can Move from Battlefield to Battlefield.
    * 737. **Hidden** (Discretionary): Pay cost to Hide facedown at a Battlefield. Gains **Reaction** next turn.
    * 738. **Legion** (Conditional): Active if you have played another Main Deck card this turn.
    * 739. **Reaction** (Permissive): Can be played/activated during **Closed States** (Chain). Also grants **Action**.
    * 740. **Shield [X]** (Passive): While defending, I have +X Might.
    * 741. **Tank** (Passive): Must be assigned lethal damage before others.
    * 742. **Temporary** (Triggered): Dies at start of next Beginning Phase.
    * 743. **Vision** (Triggered): On enter, look at top card of deck, may recycle.
    * 744. **Equip [Cost]** (Activated): Attach to a unit.
        * 744.3. **Equipped**: State of having Equipment attached.
    * 745. **Quick-Draw** (Triggered/Permissive): Equip speed is Reaction.
    * 746. **Repeat [Cost]** (Optional Cost): Pay to execute spell effect twice.
    * 747. **Weaponmaster** (Triggered): On play, cheat out Equipment at discount.
