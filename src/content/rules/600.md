## 600. Ending the Game

### 649. Conceding
650. A player may concede at any time.
651. When a player concedes, they are removed from the game.
    * 651.1. If only one player remains, they **Win**.
    * 651.2. If more than one remains, follow **Removal of a Player**.
    * 651.4. In Team modes, if a player concedes, the team loses.

### 652. Removal of a Player
1. **Banish** all their permanents/runes.
2. Remove their **Battlefield** (replace with token if in use).
    * 652.2.c. Continuous effects from that battlefield cease.
3. Remove their cards from the game.
4. **Counter** their spells/abilities on the chain.
5. Proceed with game:
    * Turn passes if they were Turn Player.
    * Focus passes.
    * Priority passes.
